##  Internship Experience

### OCP — HSE Management Project
**Internship Project | May 2026**

During my internship at **OCP**, I worked on an HSE (Health, Safety and Environment) Management application requested by the responsible person at the organization.

The objective was to develop a digital solution to help manage and monitor HSE activities.

**Key features included:**
- Accident and near-miss tracking
- Regulatory controls and safety deviations
- External company management
- Equipment and safety training management
- Employee authorizations and habilitations
- Weekly HSE KPIs and dashboards
- User management and activity history

**Technologies used:**
- React.js for the frontend
- Laravel for the backend
- REST APIs for communication between frontend and backend
- Database management and Git/GitHub for version control

This project helped me strengthen my full stack development skills and understand how digital solutions can support operational safety management.
